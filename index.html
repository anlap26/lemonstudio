Questions

FAQ

What do I get in 7 days?

A finished website on your domain: layout, texts, photos, a mobile version and buttons for WhatsApp or Viber. The clock starts after we have your brief, logo and access to the domain.

Do I need to pay for hosting?

No. We publish the site on GitHub Pages, which is free and fast. You only pay for the domain, usually about €10–15 a year.

Can the site be in Russian, English and Greek?

Yes. The one-page package starts in one language. The business and premium packages can include more.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?

Yes, on the business and premium packages. We set up a simple admin panel so you can change texts and photos without code.

Do I already need a domain and photos?

A domain can be bought later, we can launch on a temporary address. If you do not have photos yet, we will agree on a practical option: your phone photos, or free-licence images, clearly labelled.

How do we pay?

A deposit to start, the rest when the site is ready to go live. We will confirm the steps in writing before work begins.
